# Business Model Canvas
## Internal Talent Marketplace — "OpportuniTee" (working title)
**Organization:** HR & Org. Effectiveness | Leadership Program AI Project
**Date:** June 2026

---

## 1. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Traditional career ladders are linear, opaque, and often inaccessible to employees who don't know the right people. Internal talent goes underutilized while managers struggle to staff short-term projects. The result: disengaged employees, attrition risk, and missed internal mobility opportunities.

---

## 2. BUSINESS MODEL CANVAS

### CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
| Segment | Who They Are | Key Pain |
|---|---|---|
| **Employees** | All staff seeking growth, stretch, or change | "I don't know what's available or if I qualify" |
| **Managers / Project Owners** | Leaders needing project support or succession-ready talent | "I can't easily find internal talent for short-term needs" |
| **Recruitment Team** | HR staff managing internal vacancy postings | "Internal mobility pipelines are manual and informal" |
| **HR / L&D Leaders** | Org effectiveness and talent development stakeholders | "We lack visibility into internal talent movement and skill gaps" |
| **Senior Leadership** | Executives sponsoring the program | "We need data on ROI of internal mobility programs" |

---

### VALUE PROPOSITIONS
| For | Value Delivered |
|---|---|
| **Employees** | Transparent view of internal opportunities; agency over their own career growth; skill-building through exposure; no need to "know someone" |
| **Managers** | Fast access to matched internal talent for gigs and immersions; reduces dependency on external hiring for short needs |
| **Recruitment** | Structured internal vacancy process; reduces time-to-fill; builds internal pipeline |
| **HR / L&D** | Skill gap visibility; mobility tracking; feedback data for development planning |
| **Leadership** | Demonstrates ROI of internal talent programs; supports culture of growth and retention |

---

### KEY FEATURES (mapped to your 6 requirements)

| # | Feature | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mindset Page** | Landing experience that reframes career growth — not just promotions, but lateral moves, gigs, immersions, and skill-building. Includes stories, definitions, and a call-to-action to explore. |
| 2 | **Opportunity Board** | Recruitment posts internal vacancies; managers post gigs (1 day–3 months); immersion openings. Each post includes role/project details, duration, time commitment, and key skills needed. |
| 3 | **Employee Profile** | Self-declared aspirations, top skills, and skills they want to develop. Searchable by managers/HR. |
| 4 | **Smart Matching** | System suggests relevant opportunities to employees (and relevant candidates to managers) based on profile-to-opportunity skill alignment. |
| 5 | **Dashboard & Reports** | HR/leadership view: matches made, applications, completions, skill coverage, engagement trends. |
| 6 | **Feedback Loop** | Post-experience feedback for both employee and manager/host. Structured rating + open comment. Feeds into profile and reporting. |

---

### CHANNELS
- Internal intranet / company portal (embedded or linked)
- Email notifications for new matches, opportunity alerts, feedback reminders
- Manager briefings / HR communications to drive adoption
- Leadership program showcase (initial launch vehicle)

---

### CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIPS
- Self-service (employees browse and apply independently)
- Automated nudges (match alerts, feedback reminders)
- HR-mediated (recruitment team curates vacancy postings)
- Community-driven (feedback ratings build trust over time)

---

### REVENUE STREAMS / VALUE METRICS
*(This is an internal platform — "revenue" = organizational value)*

| Metric | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| # of internal matches made | Core output of the platform |
| # of gigs / vacancies filled internally | Demonstrates reduced external hiring cost |
| Employee engagement scores (pre/post) | Validates impact on morale and retention |
| Skill gap closure rate | Shows L&D effectiveness |
| Feedback ratings (employee + manager) | Quality signal for opportunities and experiences |
| Time-to-fill for internal vacancies | Efficiency metric for Recruitment |

---

### KEY RESOURCES
- **Platform:** Web application (see Tech Blueprint)
- **Data:** Employee profiles, opportunity postings, match history, feedback
- **People:** HR admin to moderate postings; champion network to drive adoption
- **AI/Logic:** Matching algorithm (skill-based)
- **Governance:** Policy on who can post, what types of gigs are eligible, confidentiality rules

---

### KEY ACTIVITIES
- Maintain and moderate opportunity postings
- Onboard employees to create profiles
- Run matching engine
- Generate and distribute reports
- Collect and act on feedback
- Communicate platform existence and value internally

---

### KEY PARTNERSHIPS
- **IT / InfoSec:** Hosting, access control, data privacy compliance
- **L&D Team:** Align platform with existing development programs
- **Business Unit Leaders:** Buy-in to post gigs and accept immersion participants
- **Communications Team:** Internal marketing of the platform
- **Leadership Program Sponsor:** Executive backing for credibility

---

## 3. ASSUMPTIONS & RISKS

| Assumption | Risk if Wrong |
|---|---|
| Employees will self-declare skills honestly | Profiles may be incomplete or inflated |
| Managers will post gigs consistently | Platform becomes empty; low adoption |
| Leadership supports releasing employees for gigs | Line managers may block participation |
| Matching algorithm is accurate enough | Poor matches erode trust in the system |
| Feedback will be candid | Social pressure may produce hollow ratings |

---

## 4. SUCCESS CRITERIA (Prototype Phase)

- [ ] At least 3 opportunity types can be posted and browsed
- [ ] Employee profile creation works end-to-end
- [ ] At least 1 matching result is generated per profile
- [ ] Dashboard shows basic match and application data
- [ ] Feedback form is completed after 1 simulated experience
- [ ] Mindset page is accessible and readable on desktop

---

## 5. PHASED ROLLOUT VISION

| Phase | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Phase 0 (Now):** Prototype | Core features, limited users, manual data, demo-ready |
| **Phase 1:** Pilot | 1–2 business units, real postings, HR-moderated |
| **Phase 2:** Scale | Company-wide, integrations with HRIS, automated matching |
| **Phase 3:** Intelligence | Predictive career pathing, skill gap analytics, retention signals |
