# Growth — 60-second demo film
**Narration script and shot list.** 143 words · 60 seconds · ~143 wpm.

## Delivery direction
Unhurried and warm. Think natural-history documentary, not advertisement: the
pace of someone describing something they find genuinely interesting, with a
beat of silence after each full stop. Drop pitch slightly on the last three
words of each section. Never sell — the numbers do that. Record at 48 kHz in a
soft room; a duvet over the mic beats any plugin.

Two lines carry the argument and should be given the most air:
*"You correct it. You don't write it."* and *"A stretch is a choice, not a
permission."* Leave a full second before each.

## If you would rather not narrate it live
The film is captioned, so it plays silently. To add voice, record the column
above against the timings, or run it through a licensed text-to-speech voice
and lay the audio under the video. Either way the cut does not change.
