01  [9.3s]  Every year, Home Credit fills more than eight in ten roles from outside. The talent was already inside. It just had no way to be found.
02  [8.6s]  Growth changes that. Four ways to move: a short gig, a developmental immersion, a service you offer the company yourself, and a permanent vacancy.
03  [9.3s]  One path runs through all of them. Build a profile, get matched, apply, your manager signs off, do the work, and it goes on your record.
04  [7.1s]  It starts with someone posting. A title, the work, and the skills it needs. Ten minutes, no requisition, no recruiter.
05  [5.4s]  Nothing goes live until HR approves it. Every post is reviewed before anyone sees it.
06  [6.1s]  And nobody starts with an empty profile. HR has already loaded what the company knows about you.
07  [9.3s]  Every skill shows where it came from. Verified by a manager, uploaded by HR, or added by you. You correct it. You don't begin from scratch.
08  [8.2s]  Then every opportunity is scored against that profile, and it shows its reasoning. You see why you matched, and what you are missing.
09  [6.4s]  And you don't have to come looking. Like LinkedIn, your top matches arrive in your inbox every week.
10  [4.6s]  The score never blocks you. A stretch is a choice, not a permission.
11  [6.8s]  Applying takes one click, and your manager releases the time before anything starts. Nobody disappears from their day job.
12  [7.5s]  You always know where you stand. In review, conversation scheduled, accepted. And if it is a no, you are told why.
13  [7.1s]  The host sees what each applicant has actually finished, and who vouched for it. Evidence, not who they already know.
14  [7.1s]  When the work ends, the host rates it, and that rating verifies the skill on your record. The loop closes.
15  [5.0s]  Managers see their whole team's growth in one place, and who needs a nudge.
16  [5.4s]  HR sees the whole company. What is posted, who is moving, and what it saved.
17  [7.9s]  Sixteen internal hires. Five point six million pesos we did not spend on recruitment. This is Growth. Where talent meets opportunity, intelligently.
